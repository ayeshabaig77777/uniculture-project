Pakistan Cultural Heritage

Exploring traditions, heritage, languages, and lifestyle of Pakistan.

Traditional Culture

Pakistan has a rich cultural heritage influenced by regional traditions, clothing styles, and family values. Each province represents a unique cultural identity.

Languages

Major languages include Urdu, Sindhi, Punjabi, Pashto, and Balochi. These languages reflect cultural diversity and regional history.

Food & Lifestyle

Traditional Pakistani food includes Biryani, Nihari, Karahi, and Sajji. Food represents hospitality and cultural warmth.
